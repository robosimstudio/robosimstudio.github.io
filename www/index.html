How it works

RoboSimStudio takes a smartphone video of a robot performing a task, here wiping a bar with a sponge, and a one-line prompt to a coding agent.

A Claude or Codex agent builds the simulation from the video: the scene, the camera and the physics, in about an hour (Real2Sim).

The output is a multi-physics simulation: rigid bodies in Newton coupled with the FEM sponge in SuperDex, which humans and agents inspect and tune until it matches the video.

We host a live simulation on a GPU: drag the gripper to move the arm, drag empty space to orbit, or pick another task from the snapshots.

Varied in its physical parameters and layout, the simulation improves a Graph-as-Policy from feedback, and the policy runs on the real robot (Sim2Real).
