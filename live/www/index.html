Real2Sim Case Studies

Each demo below is the live simulation, embedded. Press Drive the robot to take the robot's hand; until then the page scrolls past it. Convert the real video to RSS

Two tasks rebuilt in RSS from videos of the real robot: wiping a grab bar and peeling the film off a phone.

1.1 · Contact-rich wiping

Wipe the bar

A chrome grab bar bolted into two black blocks, a blue marker stripe along its crown, and a kitchen sponge. The right arm presses and wipes while the left arm steadies the bar. The stripe comes off through a removal field fed by the sponge's own contact patch, so only real pressure cleans. The scene is the twin of a real clip, rebuilt by a coding agent.

How to drive

Until you grab it, the robot runs a preprogrammed motion. When you grab the hand and it turns blue, you're driving.

  • SpongeDrag it down to press it onto the bar, sideways to wipe.
  • ViewDrag empty space to turn; right-drag or two fingers to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • ButtonsNew stripe re-inks the bar; Reset view brings the camera back.

Wipe the blue stripe off. The panel shows the press force and the peak contact pressure.

1.2 · Adhesive peeling

Peel the film

The protective film on a phone, held down by a field of adhesive bonds after AutoPeel (ICRA 2025). The left hand holds the phone; the right has the film's tab and pulls it along the peel arc. Each bond lets go when it is stretched past its limit, one row at a time.

How to drive

Until you grab it, the robot runs a preprogrammed motion. When you grab the hand and it turns blue, you're driving.

  • HandDrag it down the phone's face to peel the film off. It plays in slow motion.

Peel the whole film off. The panel's map shows each bond as it stretches and breaks.

Extend the scenario to other deformable objects

The same toolkit on a deformable the videos don't show: a cable laid into clips.

2.1 · Cable routing

Route the cable

A 45 cm, 5 mm cable runs from a socket; lay it into three Y-prong clips. The cable is SuperDex's Cosserat rod, and every rigid shape it can touch is mirrored into SuperDex, so the cable feels the arm, the bench and the clips that Newton simulates. The hand holds the cable's end with node constraints re-aimed every substep.

How to drive

Until you grab it, the robot runs a preprogrammed motion. When you grab the hand and it turns blue, you're driving.

  • HandDrag it: up lifts the cable, sideways carries it along the clips.
  • ShadowDrag the hand's shadow on the bench to move it in or out.
