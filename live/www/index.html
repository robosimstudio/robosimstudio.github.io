Robot Sim Studio (RSS) Real2Sim case studies

Four case studies, simulated live

Four robot tasks, each simulated live on its own GPU: not recordings. Pick one and drive the robot yourself.

  • 1Drag the robot's hand (on Wipe the bar, the sponge). It follows at its real speed limit.
  • 2Drag empty space to turn the view, right-drag or two fingers to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Reset view brings the camera back.
  • 3One person drives at a time; everyone else watches the same simulation. New … starts the task over.
